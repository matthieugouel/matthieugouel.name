Hey! 👋 I'm currently a Senior Systems Engineer at [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com), France, where I lead the full lifecycle (design, development, and day-to-day operation) of the services that collect, store, and surface network data (BMP/BGP, sFlow, RPKI, network device states, …). This infrastructure is used by network engineers to troubleshoot and operate Cloudflare's global network, and by automation systems to drive proactive and remediative changes. I'm also actively shaping the next generation of network automation by building foundational AI-powered tooling to reduce toil, accelerate workflows, and enable more reliable network operations.

I also run [nxthdr](https://nxthdr.dev), a French non-profit (*association loi 1901*) building open infrastructure for Internet experimentation. It operates a global network infrastructure for research and education, offering flexible worldwide active measurements from our own autonomous system and peering capabilities through multiple Internet exchanges. The whole platform is designed for rapid experimentation, education, and reproducibility, and built on three principles: open data, open source, and radical transparency.

I completed my [PhD](/posts/2023/phd-thesis/) at [Sorbonne Université](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/en) in 2023. My [doctoral work](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04164622v1/document) is centered on advancing our understanding of the internet infrastructure, achieved through the application of internet-wide high-speed measurements and extensive large-scale analysis. Broadly speaking, my research contributes to addressing challenges across diverse research domains, including network topology and performance, network security, and traffic engineering.

Previously, I held positions as a Software Engineer at the [French Ministry for the Armed Forces](https://www.defense.gouv.fr) and as a DevOps Engineer at [Société Générale](https://www.societegenerale.com/en).
